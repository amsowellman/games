How to Play

🖱 Desktop: WASD to move, mouse to look, click to throw.
📱 Mobile: left stick to move (auto-aim!), 🏐 button to throw.

🤾 Dodge the animals' dodgeballs! Throw back to score points.
🍕 Pizza heals you. 🍦 Ice cream makes you super fast.
🦒🦨 Giraffes and ferrets are worth bonus points (and make a funny sound!).
🌈 Grab power-ups, then press SPACE (or tap the power-up button) to unleash them! Survive the zoo, chase them to the mountains, then face... the DRAGON!
🏆 Crack the top 10 and put your initials on the leaderboard!
