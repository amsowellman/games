# STAR BED 🌟🛏️

A cozy space game where the "monsters" aren't monsters at all — they're lost
baby space creatures who think the glowing Star Bed is their mama! You guard
the bed by flinging a gentle net at them (pull back with your thumb, Angry
Birds style, and watch the dotted trajectory). Every critter you catch gets
scooped up, wiggles happily, and flies off to live in the Pet Pen, where they
bounce, nap, chase each other, and say "boing" when you tap them. Nothing ever
gets hurt — when a baby nibbles the bed, it just costs a heart and the baby
bounces away smiling.

**Game director: Liam** ⭐ — Liam designed the whole thing: the story of the
lost babies, the slingshot net, the tricky-but-fair movement patterns
(sine-weavers, wide circlers, blinkers, and bouncers that ricochet off moon
rocks), the critter roster (Bloop the mascot, Zippy, Chonk, Spiker,
Invisibloop, and the rare Grandpa Nebula), and the rule that everyone gets a
safe home in the Pet Pen. This build was made to Liam's spec.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser — it's a single self-contained file
(canvas-drawn art, WebAudio sounds, no downloads). Or host the folder on
GitHub Pages (it has `index.html` at the root, plus a tiny service worker so
it keeps working offline after the first visit).

- **Pull & release** near the bottom of the screen to fling the net.
- Missed nets come back on their own after half a second — no punishment, just try again.
- Best wave and critters-saved records, and the Pet Pen roster, are saved in your browser (localStorage).
